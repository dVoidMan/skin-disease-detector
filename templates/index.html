How the Screening Pipeline Works

A three-stage streamlined workflow from capture to actionable medical insights

1
Image Capture & Preprocessing

The lesion photo is validated, standardized to a 224x224 RGB tensor, and normalized for color constancy.

2
Deep Learning Inference

The convolutional neural network evaluates spatial features and generates a probability distribution across 8 disease classes.

3
Clinical Insights & Advice

Immediate presentation of the top condition, confidence level, self-care precautions, and red-flag warning signs.
