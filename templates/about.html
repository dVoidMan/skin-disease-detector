Project Motivation & Goal

Skin diseases are among the most pervasive health burdens worldwide, yet certified dermatologists remain critically inaccessible in many low- and middle-income regions, including parts of Nigeria.

DermAI demonstrates the power of applied machine learning: utilizing lightweight, transfer-learned convolutional neural networks to deliver fast, accessible visual screening, triaging high-risk lesions like melanoma, and providing medically reviewed self-care knowledge.

Technical Highlights
  • Deep Learning Core: MobileNetV2 CNN architecture with transfer learning optimized for fine-grained lesion classification.
  • Clinical Safety Guardrails: YCbCr skin tone segmentation & structural edge complexity analysis to reject non-skin photos and cluttered room scenes.
  • Dual Cloud/Local Storage: S3-compatible Cloudflare R2 bucket integration with seamless local storage fallback.
  • Production Ready: Docker and Docker Compose containerization for portable cross-platform deployment.
